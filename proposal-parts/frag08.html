Section 8: Engagement Modes

THREE LEVELS OF ATTENTION

Every engagement we run is built around one question: who is paying attention to your firm, and how do you turn that attention into business?

There are three levels of attention worth building toward. Each engagement mode is designed to reach a different one.

LEVEL 1: AI mentions you. The technical foundation that gets CTW cited by AI search engines when prospects ask category questions. Schema, structured content, FAQ architecture, plain-language service pages. This is the floor of modern discovery and the goal of the Path Forward Foundation tier.

LEVEL 2: AI recommends you. The sustained content production that moves CTW from "mentioned" to "recommended" on the answer engines that drive the largest share of prospect research. Press releases, podcast transcripts, original research, partner-bylined content, repurposed video. The algorithms start naming you first. This is the work of the Path Forward Engine tier.

LEVEL 3: People talk about you. The human attention that AI has not yet replaced and won't anytime soon. Podcast guests becoming referral sources. Partners building real audiences on LinkedIn. Original research getting cited in trade press. A network effect that compounds for years. This is what the Path Forward Partnership tier exists to produce.

CTW can stop at any of the three levels. Most firms in the category aren't operating past the first.
